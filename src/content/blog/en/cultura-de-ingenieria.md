A great security architecture doesn't get you far if teams don't adopt the practices that sustain it. Part of my role today is exactly that: leading engineering culture transformation across the organization, alongside the purely technical work.

## Culture and metrics aren't separate topics

One lesson I keep coming back to: **culture without data is opinion, and data without culture doesn't change behavior**. That's why, alongside culture initiatives, we closely track technical metrics — remediation times, tooling adoption, code quality, and more. Metrics tell us where we stand; culture determines whether the team actually moves toward where we want to go.

## AI as an accelerator, not a replacement for the conversation

Adopting AI — like using Copilot's agent mode to remediate vulnerabilities — doesn't replace the cultural work. If anything, it makes it more visible: when a tool speeds up the technical work, the conversation quickly shifts to how the team evaluates, trusts, and takes ownership of those results. That's a culture problem, not a technology one.

## What's next

I'll keep sharing what I learn along this path here — both the technical lessons and the organizational-culture ones — hoping they're useful to other teams facing similar challenges.
